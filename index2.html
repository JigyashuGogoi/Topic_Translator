Tea Garden

Welcome to Assam Tea Garden

Assam is famous for producing one of the finest teas in the world. The lush green tea gardens attract tourists from different countries and play an important role in India's economy.

History

Tea cultivation in Assam began during the British period and later became one of the world's largest tea industries.

Importance

Tea contributes to employment, tourism, exports and economic growth while supporting millions of workers.

Tourism

Visitors enjoy beautiful landscapes, tea tasting, cultural experiences and peaceful natural surroundings.
